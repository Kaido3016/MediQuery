# Step 18 — Negotiation & LOI Framework

## Objective

Move a qualified buyer from interest to a structured transaction without prematurely transferring assets or overpromising.

## Negotiation sequence

1. Confirm strategic fit.
2. Establish confidentiality where appropriate.
3. Demonstrate the exact release.
4. Complete technical/IP diligence.
5. Agree on transaction scope.
6. Discuss valuation and structure.
7. Negotiate a non-binding LOI when appropriate.
8. Complete definitive legal documentation.
9. Transfer assets only according to the executed agreement.

## Commercial variables

- purchase price;
- cash versus contingent consideration;
- transition/support period;
- employment or consulting arrangement if separately desired;
- warranties and representations;
- indemnification;
- escrow/holdback if applicable;
- exclusivity period;
- closing conditions;
- transferred versus excluded assets.

## LOI outline

A counsel-reviewed LOI may identify:

- parties;
- transaction structure;
- indicative consideration;
- scope of assets;
- proposed transition;
- diligence period;
- confidentiality;
- exclusivity, if any;
- conditions to closing;
- treatment of expenses;
- non-binding versus binding provisions.

## Important rule

Do not use a generic template as a substitute for transaction counsel. The repository documents are business/technical preparation materials, not legal advice.

## Exit criterion

A serious buyer can move from technical diligence to a clearly scoped commercial negotiation with no ambiguity about what is being sold.
