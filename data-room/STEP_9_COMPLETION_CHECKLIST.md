# MediQuery — Step 9 Completion Checklist

## Objective

Create a concise executive acquisition package that can be shared with a qualified prospective buyer after appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

## Completed

- [x] Created an executive acquisition one-pager.
- [x] Defined the acquisition thesis in business language.
- [x] Listed concrete existing capabilities.
- [x] Explained buyer value without claiming a technical moat that is not evidenced.
- [x] Defined the ideal strategic buyer profile.
- [x] Documented a defensible $100K+ commercial positioning framework.
- [x] Explicitly separated negotiated acquisition price from unsupported intrinsic valuation claims.
- [x] Added a clear limitations/claims policy to the executive material.
- [x] Documented the transaction path from outreach through technical transition.
- [x] Connected the package to the existing acquisition data-room and contact-tracking process.

## Exit criteria

Step 9 is complete when a qualified executive can understand, in a few minutes:

1. What MediQuery is.
2. What is actually built.
3. Why it may be strategically useful.
4. What the buyer would need to add.
5. How a transaction could proceed.
6. Why a $100K+ price can be negotiated without making unsupported technical or regulatory claims.

All six criteria are addressed by `EXECUTIVE_ONE_PAGER.md` together with the existing acquisition materials.

## Important use rule

The one-pager is an acquisition document, not a public claim of customer traction, regulatory certification, clinical validation, or guaranteed valuation. Any external version must be updated with verified buyer-specific facts before distribution.
