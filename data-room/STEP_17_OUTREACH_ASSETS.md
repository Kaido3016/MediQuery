# Step 17 — Buyer Outreach Assets

## Objective

Create concise, truthful messages for the first contact and follow-up stages.

## First-contact message

> I built MediQuery, a healthcare-oriented medical-report software foundation focused on secure report ingestion, authenticated access, owner isolation, evidence-preserving extraction, testing, and documented security/deployment patterns. I'm exploring a strategic sale to a health-tech company that could reuse the foundation and extend it with its existing AI and clinical infrastructure. If this aligns with your roadmap, I can share a short acquisition brief and technical demo under appropriate confidentiality.

## Follow-up after interest

> Thanks for the interest. I can provide the executive acquisition brief, the reproducible demo runbook, and the technical evidence matrix. I want to be explicit that MediQuery is being offered as a software foundation rather than as a clinically validated or regulatory-certified medical AI product.

## Executive call opener

> The thesis is simple: the buyer is not purchasing a promise of unfinished AI. The buyer is acquiring an existing healthcare-oriented software foundation, the engineering decisions behind it, and potentially the builder's transition support, in order to accelerate a roadmap where that foundation has strategic value.

## Qualification questions

1. Is medical-report/document processing on your roadmap?
2. Are you currently building this internally?
3. What is the time-to-market target?
4. Would an existing backend foundation accelerate the project?
5. Do you already have AI and compliance infrastructure?
6. Who owns the product and acquisition decision?

## Outreach rule

Personalize each message around the buyer's actual product. Do not mass-message claims about clinical AI, compliance, users, or revenue that cannot be evidenced.

## Exit criterion

A qualified prospect can receive a concise explanation of MediQuery and move into an executive/technical conversation without requiring a long sales document.
