# Step 19 — Closing & Technical Transition Plan

## Objective

Make the handoff predictable after a definitive transaction agreement is executed.

## Pre-closing

- Freeze the exact release being transferred.
- Produce final source archive/repository reference.
- Confirm IP schedule.
- Confirm third-party license schedule.
- Remove or rotate any seller-only secrets before transfer.
- Confirm domains/assets included in the transaction.
- Agree transition dates and responsibilities.
- Confirm buyer acceptance criteria.

## Handoff

Provide, as contractually agreed:

- source repository/history;
- build and deployment instructions;
- architecture/security documentation;
- test instructions and results;
- acquisition data-room materials;
- known limitations and technical debt register;
- dependency/license information;
- transition sessions.

## Post-closing transition

Suggested sessions:

1. Architecture walkthrough.
2. Local development setup.
3. Test/CI walkthrough.
4. Deployment walkthrough.
5. Security/configuration walkthrough.
6. Data model/report lifecycle walkthrough.
7. Known limitations and roadmap discussion.

## Exit criterion

The buyer can operate, inspect, and extend the acquired codebase using the agreed documentation and transition support.
