# Step 15 — Privacy & Regulatory Readiness Package

## Objective

Prevent unsupported healthcare/compliance claims and give a buyer a structured path to assess the intended deployment.

## Topics requiring buyer/legal review

- Applicable Quebec privacy requirements, including Law 25 where relevant.
- PIPEDA applicability where relevant.
- Provincial healthcare/privacy requirements based on deployment jurisdiction.
- GDPR or other foreign requirements when applicable.
- Data residency.
- Consent and lawful processing.
- Retention and deletion.
- Access requests and correction workflows.
- Incident response and breach notification.
- Encryption at rest and in transit.
- Vendor/subprocessor management.
- Audit logging and access review.
- Clinical safety and intended-use assessment.
- Medical-device/regulatory classification where the final product could trigger it.

## Prohibited claims without evidence

Do not state that MediQuery is HIPAA compliant, PIPEDA compliant, PHIPA compliant, Law 25 compliant, SOC 2 certified, clinically validated, FDA approved, or Health Canada approved unless the relevant evidence actually exists and the claim is legally appropriate.

## Exit criterion

A buyer can see exactly which privacy/regulatory questions are architecture questions, which require legal analysis, and which require formal assurance evidence.
