# MediQuery — Strategic Buyer Target List

## Purpose

Define a focused acquisition prospect universe for MediQuery. This is a **strategic-fit list**, not evidence that any listed organization wants to acquire MediQuery.

## Priority scoring

- **Strategic fit:** relevance of medical documents, patient workflows, healthcare SaaS, or clinical software.
- **Capability gap:** likelihood that an existing foundation could save meaningful product/engineering time.
- **Acquisition practicality:** likelihood that the organization can evaluate a small software/IP transaction.
- **Outreach priority:** combined practical priority for initial contact.

Scores are directional and must be reassessed before outreach.

## Decision-maker roles

Prefer:

- Founder / CEO for smaller companies;
- CTO;
- Chief Product Officer / VP Product;
- VP Engineering;
- Head of Corporate Development;
- Head of M&A / Strategic Partnerships;
- Head of Innovation / Digital Health.

Do not default to investor-relations, generic support, or generic sales inboxes.

## Outreach qualification

A prospect becomes **qualified** only when there is evidence of at least one concrete reason MediQuery could accelerate a current roadmap.

Strong qualification signals:

- medical reports are already handled or planned;
- document ingestion is an identified product problem;
- the company is expanding patient-facing digital workflows;
- the company has an AI platform but needs a secure document foundation;
- leadership explicitly discusses build-vs-buy;
- leadership requests the acquisition brief or technical demo.

Weak signals that should not be treated as buyer intent:

- general interest in AI;
- healthcare industry membership;
- company size alone;
- a public partnership unrelated to medical documents;
- an employee viewing or accepting a connection request.

## Outreach sequencing

### Wave 1

Contact only highly relevant decision-makers with the short confidential pitch.

### Wave 2

After measuring responses, refine the message and approach the next group of qualified prospects.

### Wave 3

Approach larger strategic organizations once the acquisition package and buyer objections are better understood.

## Competitive process
