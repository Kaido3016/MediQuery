## Outreach rules

1. Keep outreach factual and concise.
2. Do not claim that a buyer has expressed interest until they actually have.
3. Do not claim competing offers unless real and authorized to disclose.
4. Do not store confidential buyer information in this public repository.
5. Use the approved acquisition positioning and claims policy.
6. Move sensitive diligence material to the appropriate controlled data room.
