# Step 13 — IP & Third-Party License Package

## Objective

Prepare the intellectual-property questions a buyer must resolve before signing a definitive agreement.

## Seller checklist

- Confirm who owns the source code.
- Identify every contributor and contractor whose work is included.
- Confirm applicable invention/IP assignments.
- Inventory trademarks, domains, logos, and product assets included in the sale.
- Inventory third-party dependencies and their licenses.
- Identify any code or data that cannot legally be transferred.
- Identify model weights, datasets, APIs, or proprietary assets separately from source code.
- Preserve license notices required by third-party software.
- Document exclusions explicitly.

## Buyer diligence

The buyer should independently verify ownership and transferability. Repository presence alone is not proof of legal ownership.

## Transaction rule

The definitive agreement should contain an explicit schedule of transferred assets and excluded assets, plus appropriate representations, warranties, and indemnification terms negotiated by counsel.

## Exit criterion

The acquisition package tells the buyer exactly what IP questions must be answered before closing and avoids representing repository contents as legally transferable without evidence.
